      Forgiving on purpose

      The game's motto is "rescuers need rescuing, too". So when something goes wrong, the game catches you.

      • Flat battery? You drop to slow reserve power and can still walk to a charger. The tow truck only comes if you really can't.
      • Lost an arm? Hard landings knock parts off. A marker shows where they went, they float in water, and they come home on their own.
      • Made a mess? Red pads reset just that one room, not the whole level.
      • Stuck? Press H. The first hint tells you the goal, and only the last one tells you the answer.
